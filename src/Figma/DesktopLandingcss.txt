color: 'black', fontSize: 18, fontFamily: 'Almarena', fontWeight: '700', wordWrap: 'break-word'
---
// Unlike standard temporary tattoos that sit on top of the skin, Just Tattoos use a skin-reactive ink that absorbs into the upper layer of your skin. This creates a natural, tattoo-like appearance without needles, pain, or long-term commitment.<br/><br/>Application is quick and simple. Once applied, the ink reacts with your skin and gradually darkens over the next 24 to 36 hours, revealing a realistic finish that looks like real ink.
